Legend & scoring guide
Strength / Quality Editorial 1–10. Gold ≥7, Silver 5–6, Bronze ≤4.
Strength anchors Strength encodes intensity and profile fit, not raw nicotine. Reference points from direct tasting: H. Upmann Half Corona = 3 (Cuban handmade, 3.5″ × 44). Romeo y Julieta Puritos = 4 (Cuban machine-made, 3.9″ × 26). Toscanello Nero = 6 (fire-cured Italian, 3″ × 34). Rocky Patel Vintage 1990 Half Corona = 6 (Honduran handmade, Connecticut Broadleaf). Note that this and Toscanello Nero share a 6 with very different finishes: the Toscano persists for hours, the Rocky Patel fades like the Upmann. Strength captures the peak, not how long it lasts.
Size Gold: ring ≥32 and length ≥4″. Silver: ring ≥26 or length ≥4″. Bronze: neither.
Value value = 6 − 3.5·log₂(price ÷ benchmark), rounded to 1–10. Benchmarks: Q1 A$1.75, Q2 A$2.50, Q3 A$3.50, Q4 A$5, Q5 A$7, Q6 A$10, Q7 A$14, Q8 A$18, Q9 A$22, Q10 A$26.
Risk Lowest cash commitment needed to test the profile. An in-stock taster can replace the pack price. Green <A$50, amber A$50–100, red >A$100.
Cadence Lenient: tolerates a normal pace. Forgiving: tolerates interruption and relighting. Sensitive: punishes rushing with heat.
Packaging Protected: tin or rigid box. Fragile: card or paper. Dry-cured: shelf-stable, no humidification required.
Pairing families Amplify = shared compound families, so both taste more like themselves; fire-cured, maduro, broadleaf, oscuro and heavy-fermentation profiles pair with espresso, mocha, dark chocolate, Biscoff, wafer or cola. Complement = pleasant contrast without reinforcement; Cuban blends generally, Connecticut shade and Cameroon pair with cola, citrus, margarita, agave or rum.
Laurels Three Golds = Crown. Four Golds = Gem. Crown and Gem require Strength 5 or above. Volume only pays off above the intensity floor.
Tiers Elite: Gold on both Strength and Quality. Strong: Gold on one. Noteworthy: Gold on neither. Size and Value do not affect tier — Size is a scope filter, and Value is a buying decision rather than a judgement about the cigar. Elite additionally requires Strength 5 or above.
Asterisk Stock unconfirmed. Excluded from automatic checks pending a call to the retailer.
Benchmarks

Use this as a diagnostic map. Each tasted cigar is listed separately. Select one to see which catalogue entries it informs and the review notes recorded from the tasting. Flavour reactions transfer most strongly within the same blend or closely related line; construction, draw, heat and smoke output transfer less reliably when the format changes.

Davidoff Escurio Robusto
Correlates to

Strong: Davidoff Primeros Escurio.

Moderate: Davidoff Escurio Mini Cigarillos.

Review notes

The strongest positive blend-family signal so far. The Robusto delivered excellent construction, substantial smoke output, complex sweet-peppery flavour and greater strength than Liga Privada No. 9. Primeros should preserve considerably more of that character than the Minis. The narrow Mini format should still be expected to smoke quicker, hotter and with less body.

Liga Privada No. 9 Short Panatela Oscuro
Correlates to

Strong: Liga Privada No. 9 Coronets.

Moderate: Liga Privada T52 Coronets and Undercrown Maduro Coronets.

Review notes

Elite construction, very high flavour intensity and substantial smoke volume strongly validate the No. 9 Coronets. T52 remains a drier and sharper variation, while Undercrown should be treated as a cheaper relative rather than an equivalent.

AJ Fernandez New World Oscuro
Correlates to

Strong: New World Oscuro compact formats from the same blend.

Review notes

Dark tobacco and underlying sweetness were present, with very good construction, but bitterness and harshness overwhelmed much of the positive flavour. Nicotine impact also failed to justify the aggression. This lowers the ceiling without making the blend a write-off: intensity is proven, balance is the concern.

Oliva Serie O Petit Corona
Correlates to

Strong: Oliva Serie O Cigarillos.

Review notes

The handmade taster was pleasant, well constructed and easy drawing, with good roasted-coffee notes and decent strength. Its weakness was flavour density: it never became particularly complex, intense or memorable. The cigarillos remain credible everyday smokes, but confidence that the blend can compete with the stronger catalogue entries is now lower.

Rocky Patel Vintage 1990 Half Corona
Correlates to

Strong: matching Rocky Patel Vintage 1990 compact formats.

Moderate: Rocky Patel Sun Grown Juniors.

Review notes

Excellent construction and a very smooth draw, but the mouthfeel and short aftertaste resembled the H. Upmann Half Corona—the reason the Upmann was not repurchased. Even with the darkest wrapper in the Vintage line, the flavour failed to persist, so other Rocky Patel small formats should not be assumed to deliver a lasting finish.

Rocky Patel Gold Label
Correlates to

Strong: matching Rocky Patel Gold Label compact formats.

Moderate: Rocky Patel Sun Grown Juniors.

Review notes

Very similar to Vintage 1990 and somewhat sweeter, yet it still failed to stand out and produced the tightest draw of the recent tastings. It supports confidence in Rocky Patel construction while reinforcing the recurring flavour-intensity concern. Sun Grown remains interesting because its stronger, spicier profile could solve that problem, but it is not an automatic palate fit.

Alonso Menendez Axe Charutos
Correlates to

Moderate: Alonso Menendez Gold Cigarillo.

Review notes

Axe confirmed genuinely appealing Brazilian tobacco character, with herbal and floral flavour, substantial smoke and an easy draw. Its weakness was construction consistency, particularly the soft and occasionally loose wrapper. Gold therefore gets a flavour-confidence boost, but its thinner machine-made format and milder blend prevent direct transfer of Axe's strength or smoke output.

CAO Bella Vanilla Petit Corona
Correlates to

Moderate: CAO Bella Vanilla Cigarillos.

Review notes

Construction was excellent and the cigar produced a pleasant sweet aroma with respectable flavour strength, but the advertised vanilla was not clearly perceptible. That supports confidence in construction and general sweet-profile appeal while reducing confidence that the name alone predicts strong vanilla flavour.

Montecristo Joyitas
Correlates to

Strong: Montecristo Club 10.

Weak: Cohiba Short 10.

Review notes

Joyitas improved on the milder H. Upmann profile with greater strength and pepper while retaining solid construction. The slightly tight draw also reinforces the main risk of moving into even narrower Club dimensions: heat and restriction can become more noticeable.

Romeo y Julieta Puritos
Correlates to

Strong: Romeo y Julieta Mini 10.

Review notes

Puritos remains the baseline for the Romeo y Julieta profile. Moving down to the Mini should reduce smoke density and body while increasing the risk of heat and thinness. Later tastings have also established that stronger, more flavour-dense cigars generally perform better.

H. Upmann Half Corona
Correlates to

Moderate: compact Cuban machine-made formats.

Review notes

Cream, nuts, coffee, cedar and classic Cuban aroma remain useful flavour references, but later tastings show that smoothness alone is not enough to rank highly. Its broad ring gauge, cooler smoke and handmade construction transfer poorly to narrow machine-made packs.

Curivari Fuerte Chicos
Correlates to

Moderate: Davidoff Nicaragua Mini Cigarillos and Partagás Serie Club 10.

Weak: Undercrown Maduro Coronets.

Review notes

Dry earth, pepper, cocoa and respectable strength remain a useful baseline for compact unflavoured cigars. Later tastings have made sweetness, smoke density and complexity more important, so dry pepper alone should no longer receive a major ranking boost.
